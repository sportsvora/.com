Leather Boxing Gloves and Hand Wraps

Gloves and wraps are the core of any boxing kit. We list leather training gloves, competition gloves and hand wraps. Check which glove weights and sizes are available before ordering.

  • Purpose: bag work, pad work, sparring and competition can need different gloves.
  • Fit: your hand should sit securely with wraps on.
  • Closure: hook-and-loop is quick to fasten, while laces need help to tie.
  • Care: air gloves after each session to reduce odour.

Boxing Equipment FAQs

What boxing equipment does a beginner need?

Most beginners start with training gloves, hand wraps and a mouth guard. Ask your coach what else your gym requires before sparring.

What glove weight should I choose?

It depends on body weight, training type and gym rules. Heavier gloves, often 14 to 16 oz for adults, are common for sparring. Confirm with your coach and ask us which weights are available.

Can a gym order boxing equipment in bulk?

Yes, send us the items and quantities and we will confirm availability and the total price.

Can you add our gym logo?

You can request logos and custom colours. We confirm which items can be customised and the cost.

How much do leather boxing gloves cost?

Our listed price for leather boxing gloves is $30 and competition gloves are $50. Shipping, taxes and customisation may change the total.

Do you ship boxing equipment to the USA, UK and Europe?

Delivery depends on the destination. Send your country and postcode or ZIP code so we can confirm availability, charges and timing.
