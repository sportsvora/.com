Handball Kit Size Guide: How to Choose the Right Fit

  1. Record each player's height and usual sportswear size.
  2. Measure chest, waist and hips using a flexible tape measure.
  3. Compare measurements with the current size chart supplied for the selected kit.
  4. Check jersey and shorts fit while allowing comfortable running, jumping and throwing movement.
  5. For youth squads, confirm measurements close to the order date and allow for growth where appropriate.

Sizes can vary between manufacturers and product styles. Request the relevant size chart and confirm all player sizes before approving a team order.

Handball Kits & Equipment FAQs

What is included in a handball kit?

A basic handball playing kit usually includes a jersey and shorts. Players also need suitable indoor court shoes and a handball appropriate for their age group and competition.

Can I order custom handball jerseys for my team?

Contact Sport Vora with your team colours, logo, player names, numbers, sizes and quantities to check customisation options and request a quote.

Do you offer handball kits for men, women and youth teams?

Share the required player groups and size breakdown so available men's, women's and youth options can be confirmed.

What equipment does a handball player need?

Typical essentials include a handball jersey, shorts, indoor court shoes, a suitable handball, socks and a sports bag. Knee or elbow pads may be useful for some players.

How do I choose the correct handball size?

Handball sizes vary by age group and competition rules. Check the current rules for your league or federation and select a ball size accordingly.

What should I look for in handball shoes?

Look for indoor court shoes with a secure fit, lateral support and non-marking soles suitable for your playing surface. Try them on and check comfort before use.
