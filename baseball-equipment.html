How to Choose Baseball Equipment

The right baseball gear depends on player age, position, competition rules, experience and fit. Before selecting equipment, check the requirements of your league or governing organisation.

Match equipment to the player

Consider age group, body measurements, position and playing level. A youth player and an adult player may need different sizes and equipment specifications.

Check league and certification rules

Bat standards, protective equipment and uniform requirements can differ between leagues and age divisions. Confirm the rules that apply to your competition before purchase.

Prioritise fit and protection

Helmets, gloves, catcher’s gear, footwear and uniforms should fit correctly and allow safe, comfortable movement during play.

Plan a complete team order

Collect each player's sizes, preferred jersey details and equipment requirements in one roster to reduce omissions and simplify the quotation process.

Baseball Jersey & Pants Size Guide

Uniform sizing can vary by style and manufacturer. Measure each player and compare the measurements with the size chart for the exact product before confirming the order.

Jersey measurements

Record chest, body length and any other measurements requested by the product size chart. Consider whether players prefer a close athletic fit or additional room over base layers.

Pants measurements

Record waist, inseam and any additional measurements shown on the selected pants chart. Check the preferred pant length and cut for each player.

Youth and adult rosters

Keep youth and adult sizes clearly separated in the roster. Include player names beside sizes to make team-order checking easier.

Always use the current size chart for the specific item. If you are unsure, send the measurements and product reference to Sport Vora before confirming quantities.
