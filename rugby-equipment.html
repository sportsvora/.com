How to Choose a Rugby Jersey

  • Fit: A close fit looks smart and gives opponents less loose fabric to grab, while players can still move freely.
  • Fabric: Look for a durable, lightweight material that dries quickly and helps resist odour. Our jersey fabric: polyester, about 220 GSM.
  • Stitching: Check the seams at the shoulders, underarms, sides and collar, which take heavy strain.
  • Decoration: Our method is sublimation printing for colours, logos and numbers, with embroidery on request.
  • Sizes: Measure each player's chest and hips and share the details with us.

Rugby Protective Gear

Our range includes mouth guards, scrum caps, shoulder pads, chest guards, rib protectors, box cups and gloves. Protective gear is a precaution: it can reduce some risks but cannot prevent every injury.

Before match use, check that padded items meet World Rugby rules and your league's requirements. Ask us for the specification of any item before ordering.
