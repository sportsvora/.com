Custom Sublimated Sportswear & Team Jerseys

Full-colour sublimation is a popular decoration method for compatible performance sportswear. The process uses heat to transfer dye into suitable fabric, making it useful for detailed graphics, gradients, multiple colours, player numbers and team identity designs.

If you are looking for custom sublimated team uniforms, tell us the garment type, preferred fabric feel, artwork and order quantity. Fabric composition, garment construction and printing compatibility must be checked for the exact product.

How to Choose the Right Custom Team Uniforms

  1. Choose the sport and garments. Specify jerseys, shorts, tracksuits, training wear or a complete team kit.
  2. Confirm playing requirements. Tell us the sport, age group, competition level and any rules that affect the uniform.
  3. Prepare your design. Share team colours, logo, sponsor artwork, preferred layout and reference images.
  4. Collect player measurements. Use the size chart for the exact product rather than relying only on generic clothing sizes.
  5. Confirm quantities. List the number of garments by style, size and team. Mention home, away or alternate kits.
  6. Review the quotation. Confirm specifications, customisation, total costs, production schedule and shipping before approving.

Team Uniform Size Guide & Roster Planning

Accurate sizing helps avoid problems when ordering uniforms for a whole squad. Request the current size chart for the exact garment and model being quoted, as measurements can vary between styles and manufacturers.

  • Record chest, waist and hip measurements where relevant.
  • Check height and garment length for tops, shorts and trousers.
  • Compare measurements with the supplier's size chart.
  • Keep a roster with player name, number, garment and size.
  • Ask players to verify their details before the team approves the order.

For youth teams, collect recent measurements and check the fit with the coach or team manager. Do not assume that the same labelled size fits identically across different garments.

Bulk Custom Team Uniform Orders & Reorders

Planning uniforms for a full roster or multiple teams? Send one consolidated order sheet where possible, separating each sport, team, garment and size group. This helps clarify quantities and reduces avoidable roster errors.

For repeat seasons, keep approved artwork, colour references, logo files, player-number formats and garment specifications. Reorders still need to be checked against current product availability and production details.

Minimum quantities, sample options, artwork services, production lead times, payment terms and shipping costs depend on the product and order. Sport Vora must confirm these details in your quotation; there is no universal minimum or delivery promise stated on this page.

Can I order custom team uniforms with my own design?

Can you add player names and numbers to team jerseys?

What sports can I order custom team kits for?

Enquire about football (soccer), basketball, baseball, softball, volleyball, rugby, cricket, handball, wrestling and other team sports. Product availability is confirmed individually.

What is the difference between custom and sublimated team uniforms?

Custom team uniforms are made or decorated to a team's requirements. Sublimation is one printing method used on compatible fabrics to apply full-colour designs into the material.

Can schools and sports academies order teamwear?

Schools, colleges, academies and clubs can send their team requirements for review. Include the number of teams, garments, quantities and size breakdown.

Can I order matching jerseys and shorts?

Include both items in your enquiry and ask for coordinated colours and design details. Matching sets depend on the available product range.

How do I choose sizes for an entire team?

Use the size chart for the exact garment and collect player measurements. Prepare a roster listing each player's name, number and required size.

Is there a minimum order quantity for custom sportswear?

Do you accept custom teamwear enquiries from the USA and Europe?

How long does custom teamwear production take?

Can I request a sample before a bulk order?
