Build your player checklist

Essential Volleyball Equipment for Every Player

A practical volleyball gear checklist helps players arrive prepared. The exact equipment depends on whether you play indoor volleyball or beach volleyball, your position, the competition rules and personal preference.

01 / PLAYING APPAREL

Jersey, Shorts & Socks

Choose breathable, comfortable playing apparel that allows reaching, jumping, landing and quick changes of direction. Team players should confirm uniform colours and numbering requirements.

02 / COURT FOOTWEAR

Volleyball Court Shoes

For indoor play, look for a secure fit, suitable court traction, lateral support and cushioning. Make sure the outsole is appropriate for the playing surface and facility rules.

03 / PLAYER PROTECTION

Knee Pads & Supports

Knee pads can add cushioning during floor contact. Choose a comfortable size that stays in place while allowing natural movement. Ankle or elbow supports may be useful for some players.

04 / BALL & PRACTICE

Volleyball & Training Gear

Use a ball suitable for indoor or beach play and the relevant age or competition category. Coaches may also need ball bags, carts, pumps and practice aids.

Indoor Volleyball vs Beach Volleyball Equipment

Indoor and beach volleyball share the same core skills, but the playing surfaces and equipment needs are different. Check your event or league specifications before purchasing competition equipment.

EquipmentIndoor VolleyballBeach Volleyball
VolleyballChoose a ball specified for indoor court play and your competition level.Choose a beach volleyball designed for outdoor sand play and applicable rules.
Playing surfaceIndoor sports floor or approved court surface.Sand court exposed to outdoor conditions.
FootwearIndoor court shoes are commonly used for traction and lateral movement.Players commonly play barefoot on sand, subject to event conditions and rules.
UniformTeam jerseys and shorts should meet the relevant competition requirements.Apparel should suit outdoor play and the event's uniform regulations.
Court setupIndoor net system, posts, antennas and facility-specific accessories.Beach net system and boundary equipment suited to sand courts.

This is a general equipment comparison, not a substitute for the current rules of your federation, league or tournament.

Make a more informed choice

How to Choose the Right Volleyball Equipment

Before ordering volleyball gear online, compare the product's intended use, fit, size, materials and competition requirements. These checks can help players and team managers avoid buying equipment that does not suit their needs.

01 / PURPOSE

Identify Your Level of Play

Decide whether the equipment is for beginner practice, school PE, club training, recreational matches or competitive play. The required specification may differ.

02 / FIT

Check Sizes and Measurements

Use the product's current size chart rather than relying only on age or usual clothing size. Check chest, waist, body length and footwear sizing where applicable.

03 / SURFACE

Match Gear to Indoor or Beach Play

Do not assume that every volleyball, shoe or court accessory is suitable for every surface. Confirm the intended playing environment before placing an order.

04 / RULES

Review League and Tournament Rules

Check requirements for ball specifications, uniform numbers, libero contrast, net height and approved equipment with your organiser or governing body.

05 / QUALITY

Compare Materials and Construction

Review fabric composition, seams, fit, grip, cushioning and product care instructions. Ask for exact specifications if a listing does not provide enough detail.

06 / ORDERING

Confirm Availability and Delivery

For team or international orders, confirm stock, customisation, total costs, shipping, taxes and estimated delivery before making payment.

Common questions

Volleyball Equipment FAQs

Helpful answers for players, coaches, parents, schools and clubs choosing volleyball gear and team uniforms.

What equipment do volleyball players need?

A typical indoor volleyball player needs a suitable volleyball uniform, court shoes, a volleyball and, where appropriate, knee pads and other protective accessories. Teams also use nets, antennas, ball carts and training equipment. Requirements vary by level and competition.

What is included in a volleyball uniform?

A standard volleyball playing uniform commonly includes a jersey and shorts. Socks and suitable court shoes complete the playing outfit. Team and competition rules may specify jersey numbers, colours, libero contrast and other uniform details.

Can volleyball uniforms be customised with team names and numbers?

Customisation may include team colours, logos, player names and numbers, subject to the available products and decoration options. Contact Sport Vora with your design and order requirements so availability and costs can be confirmed.

How do I choose volleyball knee pads?

Consider fit, comfort, coverage, flexibility and how securely the knee pads stay in place during movement. Choose a size that feels secure without restricting circulation or movement.

Are volleyball shoes different from regular trainers?

Volleyball court shoes are designed for indoor court movement and commonly focus on grip, lateral support, cushioning and stability. Choose footwear suited to the playing surface and your fit requirements.

What is the difference between indoor and beach volleyball equipment?

Indoor and beach volleyball use different ball specifications and playing environments. Beach volleyball equipment should be suitable for sand and outdoor conditions, while indoor equipment is selected for court use. Check the rules of your competition before buying.

What should a school or club include in a volleyball equipment order?

A team order may include player jerseys, shorts, libero jerseys, volleyballs, knee pads, nets, antennas, ball bags and training equipment. The right list depends on squad size, age group, facilities and competition requirements.

Do you supply volleyball equipment to the UK, Europe and the USA?

International order and delivery options depend on the destination and requested products. Contact Sport Vora with your country and postcode or ZIP code to confirm availability, shipping charges and estimated delivery.

How can I request a volleyball team quote?

Send your team or organisation name, required products, quantities, sizes, preferred colours, customisation details and delivery destination. Sport Vora can then review the request and confirm product availability and the quotation.

How should volleyball uniforms be washed?

Follow the garment care label. In general, turning uniforms inside out, using a cool wash and avoiding high heat can help protect fabric, prints and decoration.
