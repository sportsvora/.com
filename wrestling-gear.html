Folkstyle, Freestyle and Greco-Roman: What to Check

Uniform and equipment rules depend on where and how you wrestle.

Where you competeWhat to check before ordering
US high school (folkstyle)Current NFHS rules and your state association
US college (folkstyle)NCAA rules; ear guards are required for live wrestling

Rules change between seasons. A singlet that suits club practice may not meet international competition requirements, so confirm the rules before you approve a design.

Wrestling Gear Checklist

For training

  • Rash guard or fitted top and shorts
  • Wrestling shoes with clean soles
  • Headgear and knee pads
  • Towel and gear bag

For competition

  • Approved singlet or uniform, in the required colours
  • Shoes with laces secured as your rules require
  • Ear guards and any other required protection

Protective gear can reduce some risks but cannot prevent every injury.

How to Measure for a Wrestling Singlet

  1. Record height and body weight.
  2. Measure the fullest part of the chest.
  3. Measure the waist and hips.
  4. Measure the torso from the top of the shoulder, between the legs and back to the same shoulder.
  5. Compare the measurements with the size chart. Ask us for it before ordering.

Youth wrestlers grow quickly, so measure close to the order date.

Wrestling Gear FAQs

What is a wrestling singlet?

A singlet is a one-piece, close-fitting wrestling uniform. It lets the athlete move freely and gives an opponent less loose fabric to catch.

What gear does a beginner wrestler need?

Start with comfortable training clothes, wrestling shoes and ear protection if your club or rules require it. A singlet is usually needed for competition. Ask your coach before buying.

Is wrestling headgear required?

It depends on the style and governing body. NCAA wrestling requires ear guards during live wrestling, and US high school rules set ear guard standards. Check the current rules for your competition.

Do wrestlers need a mouth guard?

Rules differ. The NCAA recommends one. Your association or school may have its own requirement, such as for wrestlers with braces, so check with your coach.

What colour singlet is used in international wrestling?

United World Wrestling competition uses red and blue singlets, and red elements are not permitted on the blue one or the other way round.

How should a singlet fit?

It should sit close to the body without restricting breathing, bending or movement. Send us height, weight, chest, waist and torso measurements.

How much does a wrestling singlet cost?

Our listed price is $10. Customisation, shipping, taxes and import duties may change the total.

Can we order custom singlets with our club logo?

You can request club colours, logos and names. We confirm what is available and the cost before you order.

How do you wash a wrestling singlet?

Wash it after every session, follow the care label and let it dry fully. Clean headgear and knee pads regularly to reduce skin infection risk.

Do you ship to the USA, UK and Europe?

Delivery depends on the destination. Send your country and postcode or ZIP code so we can confirm availability, charges and timing.
